About the Studio

Luxury Stay Studio

We are a property cinematics studio devoted to one idea: a beautiful property deserves to be felt, not just seen.

Luxury Stay Studio creates cinematic visual stories for luxury villas, Airbnb homes, boutique hotels, resorts and property managers around the world. We combine thoughtful direction, elegant editing and modern creative tools to turn spaces into experiences guests want to step into.

Our approach is quiet, considered and detail-led — slow movement, natural light, and the small moments that make a stay unforgettable.
